About the Workshop

Three decades of aluminium and interior work, still hands-on.

Commenced in 1994 at B.K. Pudur, Coimbatore, SBA Inteeriors is a sole proprietorship run by N. Chandrasekaran, manufacturing UPVC and aluminium windows, aluminium doors, modular kitchens, wooden wardrobes, and ACP cladding. Every piece passes through an in-house quality inspection unit before it reaches site — our team works directly with each customer to match the design, material, and timeline to the brief, without cutting corners on finish.
